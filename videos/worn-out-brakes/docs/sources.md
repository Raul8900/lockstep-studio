## Calculated (not quoted)

- Energy of the 737-10 at the moment of braking: ½ × 89,765 kg × (92.60 m/s)² = **384.9 MJ** (180 kn = 92.60 m/s). Spoken as "about 385 megajoules". Some goes to air drag, so the script says the brakes take "most of it", not all.
- Comparison kept off screen unless needed: 385 MJ ≈ 665 cars (1,500 kg) at 100 km/h; or enough to boil ~1,150 litres of water from 20 °C.
- 2,500 °F = 1,371 °C.

## Checked and not used

- 14 CFR 25.125 (landing distance) does not itself say reverse thrust is excluded, so the video does not claim that landings are certified without reversers.
- SKYbrary's Fusible Plug and Brakes pages did not load; no claim depends on them.
